Built for privileged and sensitive information

Family law files hold children's details, finances and allegations of abuse. Every system we build is designed around confidentiality first.

  • An attorney in the loop

    Nothing goes to a client, opposing counsel or the court without review by someone at your firm. Automation drafts; your lawyers decide.

  • Your data stays yours

    We set up AI tools so client information is not used to train models, and we work only with vendors that sign data protection agreements.

  • Access controls and an audit trail

    Staff see only the matters they work on, and every automated step is logged so you can show what happened and when.

  • Aligned with your ethical duties

    Our setup follows the duties of competence, confidentiality and supervision covered in ABA Formal Opinion 512 on generative AI, and we adapt it to your state bar's guidance.

Questions firms ask us

Do we need to change our case management software?

No. We build around the practice management, document and calendar tools you already use. If a tool you rely on can't support a workflow safely, we'll tell you in the blueprint before you spend anything.

Will the AI give legal advice to our clients?

No. Client-facing automation sticks to status updates, document requests and routine logistics. Anything that touches advice, strategy or a sensitive situation goes to a person at your firm.

What size firm is this for?

Solo practitioners through mid-sized family law firms. Smaller firms usually start with intake and disclosures; larger firms often start with drafting and calendaring across several attorneys.

How soon will we see a difference?

We start with the one workflow that costs you the most time, so you'll see a difference as soon as that first workflow goes live, usually within weeks, not months.

How is pricing structured?

Each workflow in your blueprint has a fixed project price, so you know the cost before work begins. Ongoing support is a flat monthly fee you can cancel at any time.
